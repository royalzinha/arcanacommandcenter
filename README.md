# arnacacommandcenter
Honestly, I’m a bit addicted to Magic: The Gathering — and probably just as addicted to Figma.

I wanted to practice my skills with Figma Make by building something around one of my own interests, instead of creating yet another random study project. That’s how Arcana came to life.

The main idea behind the project is to make managing MTG decks and collections a little easier. The goal is to keep track of the cards you already own, see their current market value, manage your decks, and create wishlists for those cards you really want to get someday.

The project is still a work in progress and definitely needs some refinement, but I’m having a lot of fun building it so far.

Next, I want to dive deeper into the design side of the project — creating a proper logo and visual identity for Arcana that actually fits the concept. I’d also love to explore the possibility of adding a more dedicated marketplace experience, allowing users to find and purchase cards directly through the platform by integrating additional APIs.

For now, though, I’m just enjoying the process and seeing where this little Magic project takes me. 🧙‍♂️
